# Welcome to the Brain
This is the center of your knowledge graph.

## How to use it
1. Create `.md` files in `vault/notes/`.
2. Use [[WikiLinks]] to connect them.
3. Click a link to jump to that note.
4. Check the Backlinks section to see what else points here.
